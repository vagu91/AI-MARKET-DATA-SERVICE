# PR 29 — Stage2-v7 news reconciliation

## Result

The V7 accounting is identity-exact and disjoint:

```text
172 raw
= 60 persisted
+ 10 technically rejected
+ 102 outside scope
+ 0 exact technical duplicates
+ 0 persistence failures
```

The union contains every captured raw ID exactly once. There are no
unexplained or cross-partition IDs.

The 60 persisted records were not produced by a cap of 10 per provider.
Their provider breakdown is:

- Federal Reserve RSS: 10 persisted;
- Yahoo Finance RSS: 25 persisted;
- Google News RSS: 25 persisted.

The 112 non-persisted records comprise:

- 75 Google News RSS records skipped by the V7
  `if index >= limit` parser branch;
- 17 Yahoo Finance RSS records skipped by the same branch;
- 10 Federal Reserve RSS records explicitly outside the requested recency
  window;
- 10 MarketWatch RSS records parsed successfully and then falsely rejected
  because the fallback acquisition label `MarketWatch RSS` was treated as
  the publisher and contradicted the direct-host publisher `MarketWatch`.

## Correction

The production parsers now process every record already returned by RSS,
GDELT and Alpha Vantage. A configured limit remains a request/page-size
control and is never a post-fetch record selector.

RSS documents are fully processed. Direct RSS feeds have explicit publisher
fallbacks separate from the acquisition-provider label. Aggregator feeds do
not invent a publisher when the feed does not declare one.

When an API response fills the requested page, coverage is `PARTIAL`, with a
cursor when supported and an explicit unknown count of records not acquired.
It is not reported as complete.

Parser and recency rejections now retain a source-identity evidence object
containing the native ID/GUID, title/content identity, timestamp and URL when
the provider payload supplies them.

## V7 evidence boundary

V7 retained complete forensic lineage for the 10 MarketWatch rejections.
For the other 102 records, V7 retained the raw identity, provider membership,
raw position and reason code, but did not persist the raw RSS XML for records
excluded before normalization. Therefore their missing titles, timestamps,
URLs and GUIDs cannot be recovered offline without fabricating evidence.

The offline replay keeps this boundary explicit:

- its 172 raw IDs and provider memberships are exact V7 evidence;
- fallback content for identity-only records is labelled synthetic;
- it does not claim byte-exact reconstruction of provider payloads V7 never
  stored.

The replay nevertheless proves that the corrected pipeline has no cap at
10, 25 or 100, preserves all 172 identities, preserves distinct temporal
updates, reaches a zero-new-record fixed point on the second execution, and
produces byte-identical output in two independent sandboxes.

## Separate network conditions

These four conditions are not part of the 112-record reconciliation:

- BLS RSS HTTP 404: configured endpoint invalid or retired; source is
  `FAILED`, never `COMPLETE`;
- BEA RSS HTTP 404: configured endpoint invalid or retired; source is
  `FAILED`, never `COMPLETE`;
- GDELT `ConnectTimeout`: external transient/connectivity degradation; source
  is `FAILED`, never `COMPLETE`;
- Yahoo metadata HTTP 307: external redirect during optional enrichment; the
  acquired record remains retained and enrichment is degraded/partial.

The machine-readable per-ID reconciliation and replay are generated by
`scripts/replay_pr29_v7_news_artifacts_offline.py`.
